Our Story

A Dream Steeped
in Tradition

It all began in 2011, when founder Tiffany Craney — inspired by a passion for cooking and entertaining inherited from her grandmother, Ann — turned a lifelong dream into reality. With the encouragement of her husband Sean, she opened the Dragonfly Tea Room in the historic Blank House in Canal Fulton, Ohio.

What started as a quaint tea room has blossomed into a beloved family destination. In 2017, the family began crafting their own signature wines, and by 2019 they opened the Dragonfly Winery in a beautifully renovated property right along the scenic Tuscarawas River. Today, the Dragonfly is a complete food and entertainment destination — a place where friends gather, memories are made, and every visit feels like a special occasion.

The Winery

Dragonfly Winery

Nestled alongside the scenic Tuscarawas River, our casual winery features 11 handcrafted wines suiting all tastes — from dry to sweet. All wines are made on-site from our own recipes using juice imported from California.

Pair your wine with our full winery menu of appetizers, flatbread pizzas, oven-baked subs, and tasty desserts. Enjoy live music every Friday and Saturday evening, or cozy up by the fireplace inside.
