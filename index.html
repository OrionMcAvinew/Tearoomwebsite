Our Story

A Dream Steeped
in Tradition

It all began in 2011, when a dream became reality with the opening of the Dragonfly Tea Room in the historic Blank House in Canal Fulton, Ohio. What started as a quaint tea room has blossomed into a beloved family-owned destination featuring a beautiful tea room, winery, and gift shops.

In 2017, we began crafting our own signature wines, and by 2019 we opened our winery space right along the scenic Tuscarawas River. Today, the Dragonfly is a complete food and entertainment destination — a place where friends gather, memories are made, and every visit feels like a special occasion.

The Winery

Dragonfly Winery

Nestled alongside the scenic Tuscarawas River, our casual winery features 11 handcrafted wines suiting all tastes — from dry to sweet. Opened in 2019 in a beautifully renovated historic property, the winery offers a relaxing atmosphere perfect for an evening out.

Pair your wine with our full menu of appetizers, flatbread pizzas, oven-baked subs, and tasty desserts. Enjoy live music every Friday and Saturday evening.
