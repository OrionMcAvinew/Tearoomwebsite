Our Story

A Dream Poured Into Every Cup

Born from a passion for cooking and entertaining, the Dragonfly Tea Room opened in 2011 inside the beloved Blank House, an 1901 landmark in Canal Fulton's Historic District. What started as a neighbourhood tea house has grown into a cherished destination for gracious lunches, themed celebrations, and unhurried afternoons.

We serve the highest-quality teas alongside homemade pastries, signature quiches, and decadent desserts — all made with care and served in a warm, English Garden setting.

Why Guests Love Us

A Little Something for Everyone

🫖

Premium Teas

Each order arrives in its own decorative individual teapot with a strainer. Our tea list spans dozens of artisan blends — from the beloved Apricot Fantasy to classic English Breakfast.

🏡

English Garden Ambiance

Floral china, a stunning teapot collection along the plate rail, and the preserved original woodwork of the 1901 Blank House create an atmosphere that's warm, elegant, and utterly unique.

🧚

Children's Fairy Garden Room

A purple-and-fairy-themed private room seating up to 12 young guests — perfect for birthday parties complete with boas, tiaras, magic wands, and two crafts.

🎉

Themed Events All Year

Join our annual favourites: Easter Bunny Tea, Harry Potter Party, Friendsgiving, Gilmore Girls Party, and more — or book your own custom event.

🍷

On-Site Winery

Since 2017, the Dragonfly Winery has produced 11 signature wines alongside a menu of flatbread pizzas, appetizers, and desserts.

Curated Gift Shop
