What to Know

Booking Details & Policies

🕐

Session Length

All tea room reservations are 2 hours. Children's birthday parties are 90 minutes. Additional time may be purchased in advance at $35 per half hour.

🌙

After-Hours Events

For parties of 20 or more, we offer exclusive Saturday evening sessions from 4 – 6 PM. Contact us to check availability.

💰

Deposit Policy

A $3.00 per person deposit is required to hold your date. Deposits are applied to your final bill and are fully refundable up to 60 days before the event.

🍽️

Adult Menu Choices

For private parties, adults select from three options: Garden Tea ($11.95), Victorian Tea ($16.95), or Dragonfly Lunch Combo ($11.20), plus tax and gratuity.
