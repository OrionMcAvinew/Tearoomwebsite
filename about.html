Our Story

From a Dream to a Destination

The Dragonfly Tea Room opened in 2011 inside the historic Blank House — an 1901 landmark nestled in the heart of Canal Fulton's Historic District. It began as a dream, born from a deep passion for cooking and the art of entertaining.

From the very first cup poured, the Dragonfly became a place where time slows down. Guests would arrive for lunch and stay for hours, drawn in by the warmth of the setting, the quality of the food, and the genuine care of the family behind it all.

Over the years, the tearoom grew in reputation and scope. Homemade food, premium teas, and thoughtful hospitality became the hallmarks of the Dragonfly brand — and the community took notice.

In 2017, the family's passion expanded further: they began producing their own signature wines. By 2019, a historic property nearby was purchased and beautifully renovated to become the Dragonfly Winery — now home to 11 unique wines and a full food menu.

The Setting

A Historic Home, Beautifully Transformed

Every detail of the Dragonfly's interior reflects the character of the 1901 Blank House and the warmth of an English Garden aesthetic.

🏡

English Garden Dining Room

The main dining room preserves the home's original woodwork and architectural details. Floral china, soft lighting, and a stunning collection of teapots along the plate rail create an atmosphere that's simultaneously elegant and welcoming.

🫖

The Teapot Collection

Frivolous and fun — that's how guests describe the extensive teapot collection displayed along the lengthy plate rail. Each piece is unique, and together they tell the story of a true tea lover's passion.

🧚

The Fairy Garden Room

Decorated in purple with whimsical fairy garden motifs, this enchanting private room was designed with little ones in mind. It seats up to 12 children and transforms every birthday tea into a magical adventure.

🛍️

The Gift Shop

Browse an ever-rotating selection of premium loose-leaf teas, elegant tea accessories, and unique gifts. The perfect place to bring a little Dragonfly magic home with you.

Canal Fulton, Ohio

A Destination Worth the Drive

The Dragonfly sits in the heart of Canal Fulton's Historic District, steps from the Ohio-Erie Canal and the beloved St. Helena III Canal Boat. A visit to the Dragonfly pairs beautifully with a stroll along the canal, making it a perfect day trip from Canton, Akron, and the surrounding region.

Guests frequently visit the tea room and the canal together — a wonderfully unhurried way to spend an afternoon in small-town Ohio.
